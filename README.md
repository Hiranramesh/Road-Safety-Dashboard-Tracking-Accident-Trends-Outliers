# Road-Safety-Dashboard-Tracking-Accident-Trends-Outliers
🚦 Road Accident Analysis Dashboard

A comprehensive data analysis project to identify accident patterns, severity trends, and risk factors using real-world road accident data.

📌 Project Overview

Road accidents cause significant fatalities, injuries, and economic loss every year. This project analyzes accident data to uncover patterns across:

Accident Severity

Urban vs Rural Distribution

Lighting & Weather Conditions

Vehicle Type Involvement

Time & Monthly Trends

The goal is to support data-driven decision-making for improving road safety.

🎯 Problem Statement

How can accident data be leveraged to:

Identify high-risk locations and time periods?

Understand severity distribution patterns?

Reduce fatal and serious accidents through targeted interventions?

Improve urban traffic safety planning?

📈 Key Insights
💡 1. Slight Accidents Dominate (≈86%)

Slight: ~8,646

Serious: ~1,010

Fatal: ~344

Although fatal cases are fewer, they require focused intervention due to their critical impact.

💡 2. Urban Areas Account for Majority of Accidents (≈98%)

Urban: 9,873

Rural: 127

Urban congestion and high traffic density significantly contribute to accident frequency.

💡 3. Daylight Accidents Are Higher, but Night Accidents Are Riskier

Daylight: 7,117 cases

Darkness (lights lit): 2,828 cases

Night accidents show relatively higher severity risk due to:

Reduced visibility

Speed violations

Driver fatigue

💡 4. Cars Are the Most Involved Vehicle Type (≈78%)

Car: 7,849 cases

Vans/Goods Vehicles: 568

Motorcycles: Significant contribution

Private vehicles play a major role in accident occurrence.
